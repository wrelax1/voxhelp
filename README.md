# VoxHelp

VoxHelp is a free communication application designed for hospitalized patients who are unable to speak, including patients with a tracheostomy or aphonia.

It allows patients to communicate essential needs, symptoms, emotions and questions by pressing large, easy-to-read buttons. VoxHelp then speaks the selected phrase aloud.

## Main features

- Large buttons designed for smartphones and tablets
- French, English, German and Italian
- Separate patient and caregiver languages
- Text-to-speech using voices installed on the device
- Personal phrases and favorites
- Works offline after installation
- No account required
- No personal data collection
- Free of charge

## Use VoxHelp

VoxHelp is available here:

https://wrelax1.github.io/voxhelp/

It can be used directly in a web browser or installed on the home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

## Purpose

VoxHelp was developed to help restore simple, immediate communication between patients who cannot speak and the people around them: healthcare professionals, family members and caregivers.

## Free application

VoxHelp is and will remain free to use for patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals and healthcare institutions.
